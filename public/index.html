Who we are

Christ-centred. Student-led. Mission-driven.

KCAU SDA Church is a vibrant community of Seventh-day Adventist students, lecturers and staff. We gather to worship God, study His Word, and serve one another and the wider community.

Whether you have been an Adventist all your life or are just curious, there is a seat for you here.

  • Bible-based preaching and teaching
  • Small family groups for real friendship
  • Opportunities to serve, sing and lead
Our Mission

To love God, love people and share the good news of Jesus on and off campus.

Our Vision

A campus family transformed by Christ and ready for His soon return.

Our Values

Faith · Fellowship · Service · Integrity · Hope
